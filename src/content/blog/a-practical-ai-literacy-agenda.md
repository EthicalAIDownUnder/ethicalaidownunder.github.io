AI literacy is becoming essential for students, workers, leaders and communities. But AI literacy should not be reduced to prompt tips or tool demonstrations.

A practical AI literacy agenda should help people understand what AI can do, where it can fail, and how to ask responsible questions before using it in real contexts.

## Key questions for AI literacy

Good AI literacy programs should help people ask:

- What problem are we trying to solve?
- Is AI appropriate for this situation?
- What data, assumptions or limitations shape the system?
- Who might be harmed or excluded?
- What human judgement, oversight or appeal process is needed?
- How will we know whether the system is working responsibly?

## From awareness to agency

The goal is not only awareness. It is agency. People should feel able to question AI systems, participate in decisions about their use and advocate for safeguards when needed.

Ethical AI Down Under will use events, workshops, resources and partnerships to support this kind of practical public AI literacy.
