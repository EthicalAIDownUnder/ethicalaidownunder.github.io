Use these questions to start a responsible AI conversation before adopting, building or promoting an AI system.

## Purpose

- What problem are we trying to solve?
- Why is AI the right approach?
- What public or organisational benefit do we expect?

## People and impact

- Who will be affected by this AI system?
- Who has been consulted?
- Who could be excluded, misrepresented or harmed?

## Transparency and accountability

- Can people tell when AI is being used?
- Can affected people ask questions or appeal outcomes?
- Who is responsible if the system fails?

## Safety and review

- What data does the system rely on?
- How will we test for bias, errors and misuse?
- How often will we review performance and impact?
