This outline can be adapted for a 60 to 90 minute AI literacy session.

## Suggested flow

1. What AI is and where people encounter it
2. What generative AI changed
3. Benefits, limitations and common failure modes
4. Ethical issues: bias, privacy, transparency, labour, safety and accountability
5. Responsible use questions for individuals and organisations
6. Scenario discussion
7. Practical next steps

## Facilitation notes

Keep the session grounded in real examples. Invite participants to share where they already encounter AI and what concerns or hopes they have.

The goal is not to create fear or hype. The goal is informed, constructive participation.
